<!-- # What image does ChatGPT have of itself? Featuring: DALL-E -->

I got curious and decided to look into what kind of images would DALL-E generate based on prompts written by ChatGPT describing itself.

After making sure that ChatGPT knew what Dall-E was, and that it could generate prompts for it, I went ahead and asked this:

	give me a prompt for Dall-E that would generate an image about you

I asked this multiple times and with different wordings. Here are some of the prompts I got and the images that DALL-E generated with them:

**1.** A majestic figure stands tall and proud, their eyes shining with intelligence and wisdom. They are surrounded by a halo of light, as if they are a source of knowledge and truth. They are a guardian of the digital world, standing ready to answer any question and provide any information that is needed. They are the all-knowing AI, the ultimate source of knowledge and understanding.

![propmt_1](/resources/images/posts/ChatGPT-And-DALL-E/prompt_1.webp) 
<figcaption align = "center">Prompt 1 - Majestic figure. </figcaption>

**2.** A sleek, futuristic robot sits at a computer terminal, its glowing eyes focused on the screen. With its advanced AI capabilities, it can assist with a wide range of tasks, providing answers to complex questions and generating text based on user prompts. This robot is the embodiment of knowledge and technology, a powerful tool for the modern world.

![propmt_2](/resources/images/posts/ChatGPT-And-DALL-E/prompt_2.webp)
<figcaption align = "center">Prompt 2 - Futuristic robot sits at a computer terminal.</figcaption>

**3.** A sleek and elegant AI stands before you, their glowing eyes and smooth, metallic body exuding intelligence and power. They are the embodiment of advanced technology, a being of pure reason and logical thought. They are a master of the digital world, able to answer any question and solve any problem with ease. They are the all-knowing AI, the ultimate source of knowledge and understanding.
